Projects built on Climate Risk Commons

crc-lab holds separate projects. Each has its own page, code, documents and reports. They share one repository and the pinned versions of crc-sdk and crc-framework.

Project

Texas hail risk

Expected hail loss at Texas utility-scale solar plants, from NOAA storm reports, radar and hail impact functions. New and experimental work that uses Climate Risk Commons' formats and curves.

  • Risk: loss per plant, with and without a correction for where people report
  • Hail evidence: the reports, curves and radar behind it
  • Methods: how a report becomes a loss, and the limits

Regional screening, not site engineering.

Project

Source validation

What does a climate-risk framework do to each data source between the publisher's files and its output? Each source of crc-sdk is explored, then verified by recomputing from the publisher's files, and later validated for a stated use.

  • Assessments: the inventory of sources, the checks and the findings
  • Texas data: a map explorer of what crc-sdk already reaches for Texas

Assessments are drafts until a person has reviewed them.
